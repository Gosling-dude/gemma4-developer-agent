# R015-v13-thinking: Gemma thinking on (with thoughts kept) on R014 — evaluated on the 2026-10-09 harness

**Verdict: promotion gate NOT met (10/24 < 15/24), but R015 beats its same-harness control 10 vs 6 (4 wins, 0 losses).**
The decisive finding of this round is not R015 itself. The organizers' harness changed on 2026-10-09, and under it our
`include_thoughts: false` bundles (V1.2, R013, R014) silently run thinking **on** with their thoughts **stripped**.

## Harness change of 2026-10-09 (verified in the official wheels)
| | R012–R014 runs (swegemma 0.2.7, adk-submission 0.2.12) | now (swegemma 0.2.11, adk-submission 0.2.13) |
|---|---|---|
| `include_thoughts: false`, no budget (V1.2–R014) | thinking **off** | harness default `thinking_budget: 4096` is merged in; the resolver checks the budget first → `enable_thinking: true`, `thinking_token_budget: 4096`, **thoughts stripped from history** |
| `include_thoughts: true` (R015) | thinking on, budget ignored | thinking on, budget enforced, **thoughts kept across tool calls** (new reasoning patch) |
| undeclared tool call | ValueError ends the task | `ToolErrorPlugin` returns it to the model as an error |
| other | – | string→int tool-argument coercion, an `unescaped` edit strategy, test files stripped from the graded diff |

These were verified by compiling the R014 and R015 archives with the new official libraries (see `extra_body`). Our notebook generator and
`official_check.py` imported moved APIs; both now support either version (evaluation tooling only).
R015 attempt 1 died on that import, an infrastructure failure preserved in `evaluations/attempts/`.

## Failure analysis (R015, 14 unresolved)
- **Time budget: 13 / 14** failures hit the 5.5-min cap.
  - 5 never left the localizer: rich_3180, rich_3063, rich_3061, rich_3468, rich_3471.
  - In the other 8 the coder ran out of time after about 100 s of its own.
  - Thinking costs about 10 s per agent step (vs ~4–5 s off), and the read-only localizer now uses a median of ~230 of the 330 s.
- rich_3480 hit no cap and produced a wrong fix (as in R014).
- Wrong fixes after an edit fell to 1 among correct-file plans, but only because most failures never reached an edit.
- The R013 tool guard held: no tool-contract crashes. The new harness would also have caught such calls.

**Not a success by the gate.** What the evidence does establish:
1. Under the harness the scorer now uses, R015 is strictly better than our R014 (4–0 paired).
2. The thinking change is directionally right.
3. The dominant obstacle is now time. A two-stage localizer→coder design cannot fit thinking-speed steps into 5.5 minutes.
